TL;DR: A diffusion policy plans the task. Proximity‑driven RMPflow reacts to the person. Recovery‑aware latent guidance helps the robot resume the task after avoidance.

Abstract

A robot can avoid a person and still lose the task. A reactive controller changes the executed motion to make room for a person or obstacle, but that change can leave a learned policy in states outside its demonstration distribution: avoiding contact does not, by itself, tell the policy how to continue.

We investigate a complementary division of roles for manipulation in human-shared workspaces. A base diffusion policy encodes task intent, while proximity-driven RMPflow modifies the executed motion for local avoidance. Latent Policy Barrier (LPB) uses action-conditioned dynamics and an expert reference to guide task resumption. Our data intervention augments the original expert-plus-rollout dynamics dataset (E + R) with targeted recovery transitions (T); it does not replace rollout data. In the dynamics-side comparison, the base policy and clean expert reference remain fixed.

A reported comparison over eight starting configurations motivates start-specific evaluation rather than a single aggregate score. Trial-level records, retry budgets, and training-data provenance must be audited before attributing differences exclusively to data placement.

This page separates recorded rollouts, human-assisted data collection, and illustrative reconstructions. They are not interchangeable evidence.

Method

Task intent

A base diffusion policy maps observation history to an action chunk. Expert demonstrations define task behavior.

Reactive avoidance

Robot-mounted proximity sensing informs a continuously running RMPflow controller that modifies the executed motion.

Task resumption

Action-conditioned dynamics predicts future latent states. LPB guidance favors candidates closer to the expert reference.

Recovery Data Collection

Targeted correction data collected in post-avoidance states — not a relabeling of autonomous motion.

  1. Identify difficult states

    Use actual post-avoidance states where continuation is unreliable, rather than relying only on broad early-policy rollout coverage.

  2. Record human correction

    Capture observations, actions and future observations during targeted teleoperated recovery, retaining mode and timing information.

  3. Expand the dynamics data

    Keep the base policy and clean expert reference fixed for the dynamics-side comparison. Direct policy fine-tuning is a separate baseline.
